## PART V: REGULATORY & COMPLIANCE FRAMEWORK

### 5.1 Ghana Regulatory Requirements

#### Business Registration
- **Entity Types**: Limited Liability Company (most common for foreign investors)
- **Process**: Registrar General's Department
- **Timeline**: 2-4 weeks
- **Cost**: ~$1,000-$2,000
- **Foreign Ownership**: 100% allowed in most sectors

#### Shipping & Logistics Licensing
- **Freight Forwarding License**: Required from Ghana Shippers' Authority
- **Customs Broker License**: Required for customs clearance
- **Port Agency License**: Required for port operations
- **Process**: Application + examination + background check
- **Timeline**: 4-8 weeks
- **Cost**: $5,000-$15,000 (varies by license type)

#### Customs Procedures (ICUMS System)

**Import Process:**
1. Importer obtains Tax Clearance Certificate from GRA
2. Electronic application for Bill of Entry via ICUMS
3. Upload supporting documents (invoice, packing list, BL, etc.)
4. Shipping company submits manifest to GRA Customs
5. Customs Declaration submission via ICUMS
6. Payment of duties/charges to authorized bank
7. Inspection by GSA, FDA, or other agencies (if required)
8. Customs Examination Officer inspection
9. Payment of GPHA fees (toll pass, waybill)
10. Terminal clerk verification
11. Customs Preventive Officers document check
12. Goods release

**Timeline**: Average 14 days for import clearance (World Bank 2024)
**Cost**: 23% of goods value (compliance costs)

#### Taxation
- **Corporate Tax**: 25%
- **VAT**: 12.5% (standard rate)
- **Withholding Tax**: 15% on dividends, 10% on interest/royalties
- **Customs Duties**: Varies by product (0-35%)
- **Port Charges**: Tema: ~$200-$400 per TEU, Takoradi: ~$150-$300 per TEU

#### Foreign Exchange Controls
- **Currency**: Ghanaian Cedi (GHS)
- **Convertibility**: Free convertibility for trade transactions
- **Repatriation**: Full repatriation of profits/dividends allowed
- **Requirements**: Must use authorized dealer banks

### 5.2 Côte d'Ivoire Regulatory Requirements

#### Business Registration
- **Entity Types**: Société Anonyme (SA), Société à Responsabilité Limitée (SARL)
- **Process**: Centre de Promotion des Investissements en Côte d'Ivoire (CEPICI)
- **Timeline**: 3-6 weeks
- **Cost**: ~$2,000-$5,000
- **Foreign Ownership**: 100% allowed in most sectors

#### Shipping & Logistics Licensing
- **Freight Forwarding**: Registration with Ministry of Transport
- **Customs Broker**: Licensed commissionnaire en douane agréé
- **Port Operations**: Authorization from Port Autonome d'Abidjan
- **Process**: Similar to Ghana but in French
- **Timeline**: 6-12 weeks
- **Cost**: €5,000-€20,000

#### Customs Procedures (GUCE System)

**Import Process:**
1. Importer registers with GUCE (single-window platform)
2. Electronic submission of import declaration
3. Payment of duties/taxes via GUCE
4. Customs inspection (selective)
5. Release order issued
6. Port authority fees payment
7. Goods release

**Key Features:**
- Single-window system linking customs, port, airport authorities
- Most importers use licensed customs brokers
- Prior authorization required for some products

**Timeline**: 7-10 days (more efficient than Ghana)
**Cost**: ~20% of goods value

#### Taxation
- **Corporate Tax**: 25%
- **VAT**: 18%
- **Withholding Tax**: 15% on dividends, 10% on interest/royalties
- **Customs Duties**: 0-40% (ETLS scheme for ECOWAS: 0-10%)
- **Port Charges**: Abidjan: ~$250-$500 per TEU, San Pedro: ~$200-$400 per TEU

#### Foreign Exchange Controls
- **Currency**: West African CFA Franc (XOF)
- **Peg**: Fixed to Euro (€1 = XOF 655.96)
- **Convertibility**: Free within French Treasury guarantees
- **Repatriation**: Full repatriation allowed
- **Stability**: Very high (backed by France)
