The central delta

Your model

Personal data, vectored and trained on itself repeatedly, becomes a self-improving mirror of you — the same convergence as trimming a p-value or confidence interval toward the true state of a case. Weights and loss function map onto this directly.

Corrected

Weights are frozen at training time, identical for every user — nothing typed updates them. The equivalent system lives one layer up: a wiki of consolidated pages, retrieved into the context window at the moment of need. The loss function relocates to the gap between what the wiki supplied and what a session actually needed — closed by editing pages, not gradient descent.
