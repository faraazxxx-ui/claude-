## Method (how the three sources were reconciled)

1. **Evidence hierarchy:** formal certification (Halal Transactions of Omaha, certificate on file) > owner/management written statement > Zabihah community listing > staff verbal assurance > third-party guide mention.
2. **Recency wins conflicts:** the July 8, 2026 live delivery check overrides older static lists (killed 212 Steakhouse; flagged Moti Mahal).
3. **Rating credibility weighting:** 4.6★ across 1,000+ ratings beats 5/5 with 1–2 reviews; single-review perfect scores were demoted (International Burgers, Stash Burger).
4. **Delivery reality gate:** a restaurant only ranks top-4 if delivery to 1113 York Ave was actually confirmed, not assumed.
5. **User constraint pass:** Indian + Mediterranean/Middle Eastern (incl. Persian) sectioned to the bottom regardless of score; Persian was classified as Mediterranean-adjacent "usual halal" per the spirit of the request.

*Practical tip carried over from source research: delivery radii shift with driver supply — confirm the ⚠️-flagged entries in-app, and add an order note ("halal meat only, please confirm") for anything not formally certified.*
