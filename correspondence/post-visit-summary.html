Advice

Every day

  • Blood pressure morning and evening, seated, after 5 minutes’ rest. Write it in a diary and bring the diary.
  • Weight every morning. Tell the doctor if it rises by 2 kg or more in a week.
  • Ankle swelling — note it and mention it. Do not stop any tablet on your own.
  • Salt low; alcohol none. Keep walking daily — to the distance that is comfortable, not beyond.
  • Go to hospital the same day for fainting or near-fainting, palpitations with dizziness, chest pain, or breathlessness at rest.
  • Bring this sheet and all reports to every visit.
