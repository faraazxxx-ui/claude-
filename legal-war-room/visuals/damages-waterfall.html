Notes
  • Evidence labels per damages_model.json honesty_rules: DOCUMENTED (record in hand or hash-chained), ESTIMABLE (methodology + records identified, computation pending), ASSERTED (client statement or posture figure without independent support).
  • ANTI-DOUBLE-COUNT: paternal loans (~$900K) and living expenses (~$1M) are excluded from all tier totals (in_totals=false) — they are the same dollars as the lost-income gap the Florida contract measures; corroboration and hardship narrative only.
  • Single-recovery rule: the same lost-income dollars cannot be recovered under multiple counts; the multi-count structure widens liability paths, not the compensatory pot. Tier 2 replaces the Tier-1 lost-salary line with back pay and carries the remaining Tier-1 items as out_of_pocket_consequentials — the tiers are alternative framings, never additive.
  • Remedy channeling (post-Cummings): emotional distress never under Title VI/Rehab/FMLA (Cummings CL 6463720); punitives never under Title VI (Barnes CL 121152) or FMLA. NYSHRL and FMLA counts are NOT YET PLED — amendment required.
  • UNRESOLVED flags carried from damages_model.json: (1) operative-year discrepancy (April 14 probation: 2022 per facts_extraction vs Mar–Apr 2024 per r3/r4) — drives limitations and back-pay start; (2) Florida executed-contract question (00:13:03 vs 00:14:21) — lock down with counsel before any demand.
  • UNVERIFIED_WEB comparators (Walker v. Ascension $10M; Pizarro $1.725M; Walsh/Su v. Tara $650K) usable in mediation atmospherics ONLY with the explicit flag: unverified — confirm with counsel.
