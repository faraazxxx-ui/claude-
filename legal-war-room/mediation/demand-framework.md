## 2. Demand Construction Rules

1. **No orphan numbers.** Every dollar figure in any demand instrument carries its evidence label and traces to `damages_model.json` (Tier 1/Tier 2 line items). If a number is not in the canon, it does not go in the demand.
2. **Open with receipts, not posture.** The first number the mediator hears is the Tier 1 documented core ($1,222,000–$1,595,500) with the wire ledger ($223,800 DOCUMENTED — 38 wires, SHA-256 chain) and billing records behind it. Credibility purchased here is what lets Tier 2 land.
3. **Argue in Tier 2; escalate by strengthening components, not inflating them.** Front pay ($700,000–$3,500,000) is $0 without the vocational/economic expert (*Tarpley*, VERIFIED, CL 4680173 — adverse); distress stays garden-variety (~$100,000) without psychiatric records; the doubling ($0–$1,120,000) and uncapped distress/punitives require the NYSHRL/FMLA amendment (not yet pled — check the amendment deadline now).
4. **Deploy the $100M anchor once, correctly, in caucus** — as the carrier's tail risk with the seven Tier-3 assumptions stated aloud, never as our valuation. Pair it with the fee-shifting clock running toward the November 2026 deadline.
5. **Remedies discipline in every draft:** no emotional distress under Title VI/Rehab/FMLA (*Cummings*); no punitives under Title VI (*Barnes*). Punitive-base disclosure: the $2M–$8M band is anchored to total compensatory, but punitives ride only on NYSHRL/tort counts — recompute or disclose before external use (see damages-architecture.md).
6. **Anti-double-count:** paternal loans (~$300K/yr × 3, ~$900K, DOCUMENTED $223,800 subset) and living expenses (~$1M / 3 yr, ESTIMABLE) are corroboration of the lost-income gap, never additive lines.
7. **Identity hygiene:** defendant is United Health Services (Binghamton, EIN 16-1165049) — not Universal Health Services, not UnitedHealth Group. The $122M DOJ settlement and $500M Nevada punitive verdict are barred from every demand instrument until counsel resolves the caption-entity conflict. Misattribution is sanctionable.
8. **Unverified comparators** (*Walker v. Ascension* $10M; *Pizarro* $1.725M; *Walsh/Su v. Tara* $650K) appear, if at all, only as mediation atmospherics with the flag "unverified — confirm with counsel."

## 3. Concession Ladder (monetary), against the non-monetary spine

Sequence (all labels repeated at each step):
1. **Anchor (caucus only):** ~$100M exposure framing — ASSERTED / NEGOTIATION POSTURE, with caveats stated.
2. **Opening demand (written):** top of Tier 2 — $16,565,500 ESTIMABLE, component arithmetic attached, conditions disclosed.
3. **Working range:** concessions move within $4.34M–$16.57M only in exchange for movement on the non-monetary must-haves; each concession cites which Tier-2 condition (expert, records, amendment) remains unpriced.
4. **Client threshold check:** $40M (NEGOTIATION POSTURE — client directive) governs client sign-off conversations, not mediator communications; counsel owns the gap between it and Tier 2 explicitly with the client.
5. **Floor:** Tier 1 core $1,222,000–$1,595,500 + all five must-haves. Below this, the answer is the BATNA: trial, multiple liability paths, fee-shifting compounding monthly.

**Non-monetary must-haves (non-negotiable at any price point — the client's true first-order objectives):**
1. Full correction/expungement of the academic record (probation removed; any document bearing the alleged forged signature withdrawn; ACGME-consistent record).
2. Clean, board-safe verification/certification letters from UHSH to licensing authorities.
3. Written, USCIS-usable stipulation confirming internal exoneration, reinstatement, graduation, and favorable termination of the criminal matter (acquittal Sept 2025).
4. Neutral-or-better reference protocol; mutual non-disparagement.
5. Institutional-change component (conflict-of-interest and GME probation-procedure reform) — accountability and sustainable change, not punishment.

Trade money for the must-haves early and visibly: they cost UHSH little and are worth more to the client than the last several million of any demand.

## 5. Standing Caveats

- **No win guarantees.** Every Tier-2/Tier-3 figure is conditional on liability findings that verified adverse authority (*Horowitz/Ewing* academic deference; *Poliner*/HCQIA — a ~$33M physician judgment reversed to zero; *Tarpley*; *Rocanova*; *Payne/Turley* ratio compression) could reduce or eliminate.
- **Causation contest:** the defense will attribute the Florida loss to the arrest/prosecution as an independent cause; the instigation record (Guthrie-originated call, Nadeem texts, records handover) is what ties it back to defendants.
- **Single-recovery rule:** the same lost-income dollars cannot be recovered under multiple counts; multi-count structure widens liability paths, not the compensatory pot.
- Figures labeled NEGOTIATION POSTURE never appear in any filing, expert report, or mediator submission as verdict expectations.
