## 0. How to read the bands

- **P(survive)** = probability the claim survives dispositive motions (Rule 12 + Rule 56) in substantially litigable form.
- **P(prevail | survive)** = probability of a favorable liability finding at trial *given* survival. Multiply the two for an unconditional trial-win estimate; but remember that the large majority of civil cases of this kind resolve before verdict (ASSERTED — general litigation-statistics observation, not sourced in the research corpus; confirm the figure with counsel before quoting it) — the bands' real function is pricing the November 2026 mediation.
- Bands are subjective probabilities built on the verified-law research and the facts canon (`facts_extraction.json`, Aug 2 2026 meeting). They are honest, not promotional.
- **Known factual fault line that moves several bands at once:** the facts canon contains an unresolved year discrepancy — the April 14 probation/FMLA/arrest sequence is placed in **2022 in parts of the meeting narrative** and **2024 in the r3/r4 research assumptions**. Limitations analysis for FMLA, ADA/NYSHRL, and parts of the tort timeline flips on this. Resolving it is Update Trigger #1.

### Claim 1 — Title VI (intentional national-origin discrimination) — PLED

**P(survive dispositive): 50–65%. P(prevail at trial | survive): 30–45%.**

**Key drivers (up):**
- Private right of action for intentional discrimination is settled — *Alexander v. Sandoval*, 532 U.S. 275 (VERIFIED, CL 2620697).
- Pattern evidence is unusually rich: Iranian resident forced out via solicited complaints; Indian resident fired 6 months from graduation over written physician protests; client's statistical claim that only non-Pakistani residents are disciplined (ASSERTED — needs discovery corroboration); comparator Dr. Ahmed's 3–6-hour leave-to-firing vs. client's treatment.

Univ. at Buffalo*, 804 F.3d 178, VERIFIED CL 8442415), bad faith (*Branum v. Clark*, 927 F.2d 698, VERIFIED CL 557202), and procedural departure from the institution's own norms (*Ezekwo*, 940 F.2d 775, VERIFIED CL 566006; *Ewing*, 474 U.S. 214, VERIFIED CL 111549). The unilateral probation signature, alleged forged signature, no-notice probation, and 24-hour reversals feed all three.

**Key drivers (down):**
- **§604 employment-practice limitation** (42 U.S.C. §2000d-3 — *official-source text; unverified — confirm with counsel*): defendants will argue Medicare/Medicaid funds are not "primarily to provide employment." Counter: direct GME funding is earmarked for resident stipends/training; Second Circuit template in *AADE v. City of Bridgeport*, 647 F.2d 256 (VERIFIED, CL 8923941). This is the survival battleground.
- Title VI runs against the **recipient only** — the four physicians will exit this count; expect a partial-dismissal motion to succeed as to individuals.
- Remedies stripped: no emotional distress (*Cummings*, 596 U.S. 212, VERIFIED CL 6463720), no punitives (*Barnes v. Gorman*, 536 U.S. 181, VERIFIED CL 121152). The count carries economic loss, equitable relief, and fees only.

**Moves the band UP:** discovery proving direct GME dollars fund resident salaries (+10–15 on survival); the complete academic record showing redactions/manipulation vs. the DA-produced version; comparator personnel files (Ahmed departure, the Indian/Iranian resident files); the 2022 ten-page rebuttal email retrieved from UHS email.
**Moves the band DOWN:** a funding record showing no employment-objective federal stream; clean CCC/committee documentation legitimizing the probation; comparator files showing Pakistani residents also disciplined.

### Claim 2 — Tortious interference (Florida contract; Vermont offer) — PLED

**P(survive dispositive): 55–70%. P(prevail at trial | survive): 35–50%.**

**Key drivers (up):**
- The Florida contract was **signed April 1, before the probation** (ASSERTED / DOCUMENTED-PENDING — client statement, meeting 00:13:03; executed contract to be exhibited but not yet in counsel's hands. **Canon contradiction flag:** at 00:14:21 the client is recorded saying he had "never signed" the offer — resolve 00:13:03 vs. 00:14:21 before relying on this rail) — if execution status holds, the claim proceeds under the easier *Lama Holding* five-element test, no "wrongful means" needed (88 N.Y.2d 413, VERIFIED CL 2038313).
- Even on the prospective-relations rail (Vermont offer, rescinded May), the alleged conduct — procuring a criminal prosecution, wanted-poster circulation (independent defamation-flavored tort), misstatements to police/DA — is squarely within *Guard-Life*'s enumerated "wrongful means," which expressly lists criminal prosecutions (50 N.Y.2d 183, VERIFIED CL 2591575; *Carvel*, 3 N.Y.3d 182, VERIFIED CL 2124135).
- "Legitimate business interest" defense is weak on the existing-contract theory — *White Plains Coat & Apron*, 8 N.Y.3d 422 (VERIFIED, CL 2085978).
- Damages measure is the case's compensatory anchor: 3-yr $350K + $70K bonus ≈ **$1.05M–$1.12M (DOCUMENTED-PENDING/ASSERTED — contract not yet in hand; duration ESTIMABLE)**.

**Key drivers (down):**

Defense will also try to drag the tort under *Rocanova*'s contract rule (83 N.Y.2d 603, VERIFIED CL 2243212); answer is the third-party-contract carve-out.

**Moves UP:** subpoena to the Florida employer producing the withdrawal reason + who contacted them; Nadeem texts; proof any defendant communicated with either prospective employer. **Moves DOWN:** Florida employer affidavit that the arrest's public record alone drove withdrawal and no one contacted them; a contingency clause conditioning the contract on unrestricted licensure.

### Claim 5 — FMLA interference/retaliation — CANDIDATE (requires amendment)

**P(survive dispositive): 55–70% *if timely and events are 2024*; falls toward 0–15% if the operative sequence is 2022. P(prevail at trial | survive): 45–60%.**

**Drivers (up):**
- Textbook prima facie architecture: FMLA request filed **before** the arrest and before/at the April 14 probation; a leave *request* is protected activity (*Potenza*, 365 F.3d 165, VERIFIED CL 785859); days-scale temporal proximity alone carries causation at the prima facie stage (*Donnelly*, 691 F.3d 134, VERIFIED CL 806387).
- **Motivating-factor causation**, not but-for, in this circuit (*Woods v. START*, 864 F.3d 158, VERIFIED CL 4409966) — the plaintiff-friendliest causation standard in the case.
- Dr. Ali's recorded "I guess a fiancée is considered family" remark (facts canon 00:43:40) concedes the request's substance was received and understood.
- Individual liability against the physicians who controlled the decisions under the economic-reality test (*Graziadio*, 817 F.3d 415, VERIFIED CL 3186382) — uninsurable-feeling personal exposure is mediation leverage.
- Liquidated (double) damages presumption with employer bearing the good-faith burden — 29 U.S.C. §2617(a)(1)(A)(iii) (*official-source text; unverified — confirm with counsel*); *DaPrato*, 482 Mass. 375 (VERIFIED, CL 4626349 — persuasive) shows "honest belief" fails as a merits defense. The ~24-hour reversal and dropped investigations are strong anti-good-faith evidence.

**Drivers (down):**
- **TIMELINESS IS THE GATE.** 2-yr SoL / 3-yr willful (§2617(c), *official-source text; unverified — confirm with counsel*). If the April 14 sequence is **2024**: the 2-year window closed ~April 2026, so the count must plead **willfulness** and be added before ~April 2027 — viable but contested. If **2022**: even the willful window closed ~April 2025 — the count is dead absent a later "last event." Resolve the year NOW.
- Damages are economic-only and possibly modest standalone (probation/leave reversed quickly; residency completed) — its value is liability hook, doubling, mandatory fees, and narrative.
- Intervening-arrest causation argument; counter is that the arrest was itself part of the retaliatory mechanism (instigation evidence) and was reversed in ~24 hours.

**Moves UP:** confirmation of 2024 dates from contemporaneous records; the FMLA paperwork itself with date stamps; internal communications about the request. **Moves DOWN:** 2022 date confirmation (kills it); records showing the probation decision predated the FMLA request.

### Claim 6 — NYSHRL (national origin + disability) ± Rehab Act; ADA Title I likely foreclosed — CANDIDATE (requires amendment)

**P(survive dispositive): 55–70% (NYSHRL). P(prevail at trial | survive): 40–55%.**

**Drivers (up):**
- **This is the money vehicle.** Post-2019 NYSHRL carries uncapped compensatory (emotional distress) *and* punitive damages against private employers — Exec. Law §297(9) (*official-source text; unverified — confirm with counsel*) — on the lower *Chauca* recklessness/conscious-disregard standard (30 N.Y.3d 325, VERIFIED CL 7170278; 2d Cir. application 885 F.3d 122, VERIFIED CL 8439840), with asymmetric fee-shifting (§297(10)).
- Disability predicate is near-certain: Brugada syndrome + ICD substantially limits circulatory function and mitigating measures (the ICD) are disregarded post-ADAAA — 42 U.S.C. §12102(2)(B), (4)(E)(i) (*official-source text; unverified — confirm with counsel*); NYSHRL's definition is broader still.
- Individual aiding-and-abetting liability against Ahmed, Nadeem, Rehman, Ali — §296(6) via *Tomka*, 66 F.3d 1295 (VERIFIED, CL 705166) and *Feingold*, 366 F.3d 138 (VERIFIED, CL 785942).
- 3-year SoL, no administrative exhaustion — timely for Mar–Apr 2024 conduct if amended in 2026 (**but see the 2022/2024 year discrepancy — if operative events are 2022, the window is closed for those events and the claim must be rebuilt on post-2023 conduct such as the records-refusal, DA cooperation, and continuing licensure obstruction**).

**Drivers (down):**
- ADA Title I requires an EEOC charge within 300 days — almost certainly missed; do not build on ADA. Rehab Act adds a federal disability hook without exhaustion but *Cummings/Barnes* strip its distress/punitive remedies (VERIFIED, CL 6463720 / CL 121152), and "solely by reason of disability" causation is costly.
- Aiding-and-abetting circularity critique for individuals — NY courts are split on whether a person can aid and abet primarily their *own* violation. The r3-Q4 notes field attributes this critique to a "*Strauss* line," but that authority is **not independently verified in the corpus — no CL id exists for it; confirm with counsel before citing** (per this file's citation rule, it is flagged here rather than cited). The pleading answer stands regardless: plead UHSH as principal violator, individuals as aiders.
- Not yet pled: everything depends on winning leave to amend — check the NDNY scheduling-order amendment deadline **immediately**.

**Moves UP:** timely amendment granted; psych-treatment corroboration for distress (without it, defense argues garden-variety caps); comparator discovery. **Moves DOWN:** amendment denied as untimely/prejudicial (this would be the single worst procedural loss available — it removes the only uncapped distress/punitive vehicle).

### Claim 7 — Malicious prosecution, NY tort rail (private instigators) — CANDIDATE, DEADLINE-CRITICAL

**P(survive dispositive): 45–60%. P(prevail at trial | survive): 30–45%.**

**The clock rules everything:** CPLR 215(3) gives ONE YEAR from favorable termination (*official-source text; unverified — confirm with counsel*) — acquittal Sept 2025 → the rail against Ahmed, Nadeem, Rehman, Ali, UHSH (respondeat superior), and Guthrie **expires ~September 2026, weeks away**. The claim is not in the current complaint. Do not rely on relation-back. Confirm the exact acquittal date from the criminal record and file the amendment with margin. (No notice-of-claim needed against private parties — GML §50-e scope, *official-source text; unverified — confirm with counsel*.)

**Elements scorecard** (*Broughton*, 37 N.Y.2d 451, VERIFIED CL 5681499; 2d Cir. restatement *Manganiello*, 612 F.3d 149, VERIFIED CL 2522805):
1. **Initiation** — contested but promising: civilian liability attaches for importuning, knowingly false information, or withholding exculpatory information (*Present v. Avon*, 253 A.D.2d 183, VERIFIED CL 6162502; *Rothstein*, 373 F.3d 275, VERIFIED CL 786706). Ammunition: the call to police came from Guthrie, not the complainant; Nadeem's constant texts with the complainant's family; Rehman allegedly passing academic information to Nadeem; UHS handing records to the DA after internally exonerating him; never-produced CCTV.
2. **Favorable termination** — **locked.** Full acquittal exceeds what *Thompson v. Clark*, 596 U.S. 36 (VERIFIED, op 6329458) requires.
3. **No probable cause** — the battleground. Acquittal is NOT evidence of no probable cause (*Rothstein*, VERIFIED CL 786706 — cuts against us). If prosecuted **by indictment**, the *Colon* presumption attaches (60 N.Y.2d 78, VERIFIED CL 5686686; burden per *Savino*, 331 F.3d 63, VERIFIED CL 782199) and must be rebutted with fraud/suppression evidence. **Confirm the charging instrument — misdemeanor information vs. indictment swings this band ±10 points by itself.**
4. **Malice** — inference-based: the ~24-hour reversal of leave and arrest, all investigations dropped, retained through pre-trial supervision, plus the retaliation timeline supply improper motive; lack of probable cause permits a malice inference (*Lowth*, 82 F.3d 563, VERIFIED CL 717472). Honest flag: no verified authority holds a rapid internal exoneration *itself* establishes malice — it is argument-by-inference.

**Continuation theory backstop:** even if initial probable cause existed, defendants who kept feeding the prosecution after their own institutions exonerated him continued it without probable cause (*Lowth* dissipation principle, VERIFIED CL 717472) — but mere failure to retract may not suffice and trial testimony is witness-immune (*Rothstein* independent-judgment limit, VERIFIED CL 786706).

**Moves UP:** ANSCO/Guthrie CCTV (subpoena now — spoliation letter already warranted); Nadeem–complainant-family texts; DA file showing what defendants supplied (grand-jury unsealing motion under CPL 190.25(4) if indicted); charging instrument = information. **Moves DOWN:** missing the Sept 2026 deadline (kills the rail entirely); DA/police affidavits of fully independent judgment; indictment + no fraud evidence.

## 4. What would have to be true for ~100% victory

"~100%" does not exist in litigation and this document will never promise it. What the aspiration translates to, honestly, is: **every claim survives, the core facts prove out on documents, and defendants' rational move is capitulation.** Each condition below is checkable — treat this as a scoreboard, not a forecast. Even with every box checked, trial retains irreducible ~10–20% variance (jury, remittitur — *Turley*, VERIFIED CL 8442054; *Payne*, VERIFIED CL 820691 — and appeal; *Poliner*, VERIFIED CL 62897, is the standing reminder that a $33M physician judgment was reversed to zero on immunity).

**Procedural conditions (binary, near-term):**
1. ☐ Exact acquittal date confirmed; malicious-prosecution amendment filed **before the CPLR 215(3) anniversary (~Sept 2026)**.
2. ☐ Leave to amend granted adding NYSHRL, FMLA (willful), malicious prosecution (both rails), Rehab Act — before the scheduling-order amendment deadline.
3. ☐ The 2022-vs-2024 operative-year discrepancy resolved by contemporaneous documents **in favor of 2024** (or claims re-anchored on post-2023 conduct).
4. ☐ Charging instrument confirmed to be an **information, not an indictment** (no *Colon* presumption).

**Evidentiary conditions (discovery scoreboard):**
5. ☐ Complete academic record produced and shown to differ materially from the DA-produced version (manipulation proof).
6. ☐ Forged signature confirmed by handwriting expert against the original document.
7. ☐ Nadeem–complainant-family texts obtained, showing coordination preceding the police call.
8. ☐ ANSCO/Guthrie CCTV obtained (or spoliation established with an adverse-inference instruction).
9. ☐ UHS/Guthrie legal-team communications (crime-fraud or non-privileged channel) showing the arrest-day coordination and the ~24-hour reversal rationale.
10. ☐ DA file / grand-jury materials showing defendants supplied the false probation narrative.
11. ☐ Direct-GME funding records satisfying the §604 employment-objective test.
12. ☐ Comparator files proving the discipline pattern (Iranian/Indian residents; Ahmed's 3–6-hour firing).
13. ☐ Damages documentation completed: remaining paternal-loan wires, pharmacy records, Florida-employer withdrawal file, psych-treatment corroboration.

**Defense-collapse conditions:**
14. ☐ No HCQIA / NY peer-review immunity ruling shielding the probation (the *Poliner* risk, VERIFIED CL 62897) — win the "this was never genuine peer review" characterization.
15. ☐ Academic-deference characterization won at summary judgment (probation ruled disciplinary/retaliatory in character, not academic).

Scoring guide: conditions 1–4 checked → composite survival moves to ~90%+. Conditions 5–10 substantially checked → trial-win bands shift up ~15–20 points each and the settlement zone approaches its upper band. All 16 → defendants' rational strategy is a capitulation-grade settlement including the full non-monetary package; that is the honest ceiling of "victory," and it still is not a guarantee.
