**Theme discipline:** the story is *"we are on the right side of this — the goal is accountability and sustainable change, not punishment."* Pressure is applied through legitimate process, not threat; the injunctive/record-relief asks are framed as the "sustainable change" the theme promises.

FY2024 revenue $1.162B / net assets ~$802M — ASSERTED [unverified — ProPublica 990 aggregator; confirm with counsel before exhibiting the 990s themselves].*

**What they fear**

4. Punitive exposure on the state-law tort counts under Walker v. Sheldon, 10 N.Y.2d 401 (1961), cluster_id 5672186 / Ross v. Louise Wise Servs., 8 N.Y.3d 478 (2007), cluster_id 2032322 — and the strategic threat of an NYSHRL amendment (Exec. **Contradictions / soft tissue**
- **AF 6 vs. AF 8 (Answer, Doc. 12):** the same pleading asserts the actions were "legitimate, non-discriminatory" business decisions (AF 6) *and* that individual conduct was "outside the course and scope of employment... not authorized or condoned" (AF 8). Both cannot be true of the same acts. Whichever way they resolve it, someone loses: within-scope → institutional liability; outside-scope → the individuals are abandoned and the "legitimate business reasons" defense collapses as to those acts.
- **AF 7 same-decision defense** implicitly concedes the adverse actions occurred and that impermissible motive is in play (Mt. Healthy/mixed-motive structure).
- **The 24-hour reversal:** within ~24 hours of the arrest, admin leave lifted, internal case dropped, all investigations at UHS *and* Guthrie stopped, client asked to return to work — while internal policy (per client — ASSERTED, document demanded) made an arrest-while-on-probation an *immediately fireable* offense, and comparator Ahmed went from admin leave to fired in 3–6 hours. Non-firing is powerful *knowledge-of-innocence* evidence: the institution behaved as if it did not believe its own predicate.
- **Records conduct:** refused the client his own academic record and called security on the family; produced records to the DA (after internally clearing and graduating him) while cutting his email access; the DA-produced set is allegedly incomplete, "especially what happened with Dr. Ahmed."
- **Probation without notice** (Dr. Jones, then-incoming head of medical education and treating physician, also did not know — his "very inhumane" letter to Dr. Ali is a demanded document) plus the probation document signed by the program director only (DOCUMENTED — evidence inventory).

**Spoliation exposure — the never-produced CCTV.** Both institutions' lawyers apparently viewed the "ANSCO" CCTV footage before dropping everything; it was never produced, yet a still from it circulated as a "wanted" poster inside UHS (nurse's message — DOCUMENTED, client possession). Litigation was reasonably foreseeable no later than the arrest/leave events. Instruments: immediate **preservation letters** (UHS, Guthrie, ANSCO entity once resolved), a **records-retention 30(b)(6) topic**, and interrogatories on custody/viewing/disposition of the footage. If the footage is gone, Rule 37(e) relief up to an adverse-inference instruction is on the table (motion practice; no spoliation case verified in the research files yet — flag for R-series follow-up, confirm authorities with counsel).

**Discovery instruments (mapped to wounds)**

- **Depositions:** 30(b)(6) on (i) probation procedure, (ii) records handling/DA cooperation, (iii) retention/CCTV, (iv) scope-of-employment positions; then GC-office custodian(s) and the CEO-level decision-maker the "straight from their legal team" call implies.

**What he fears**

An uninsured, un-indemnified individual punitive exposure — even a modest one — is life-altering for a physician; individual punitive verdicts are less ratio-compressed in practice and typically uninsurable (r4 synthesis).

**Contradictions / soft tissue**
- **Answer ¶2 vs. Complaint ¶3:** admits he is a physician of Pakistani origin "formerly associated with" UHSH but denies any "position of authority and/or influence over Plaintiff." The denial is falsifiable by rotation schedules (DOCUMENTED — evidence inventory), teaching-faculty rosters, evaluation forms he signed, and the zero-patients week (normal ~10 — ASSERTED, corroborable by census records): you cannot control a resident's patient assignments for a week without authority over him.
- **Answer ¶10 admission** "upon information and belief" — likely concedes the engagement background; pin down exactly what was admitted.
- **AF 4 (personal jurisdiction) and AF 5 (improper service)** are boilerplate with no visible factual basis — demand it by interrogatory; abandonment of these defenses is an early, cheap credibility hit.
- The 2021–2022 HR complaints against him (sent from the client's UHS email — preservation demanded) and the senior resident's "please don't complain about him" texts (DOCUMENTED — client possession) establish both notice and a culture-of-fear pattern.

**Discovery instruments:** interrogatories forcing him to define "formerly associated" and enumerate duties; RFP for his personnel/discipline/departure file (to UHSH); deposition built around schedules and evaluations he signed; third-party depositions (co-residents, the mocking fellow, Dr. Janice) on his actual role. **Divergence play:** ask UHSH about Ahmed's authority and Ahmed about UHSH's program decisions — their answers cannot converge.

**Settlement-pressure rating: HIGH for non-monetary concessions, MODERATE for dollars (7/10).** Limited personal assets (ASSERTED — no asset discovery yet) but maximal narrative exposure and — if the reported disciplinary firing (ASSERTED, per client; personnel file demanded) is borne out — no institutional shield. He is the most likely early separate-peace candidate: a truthful declaration about program authority and record-handling in exchange for a modest resolution would be worth more than money.

## 8. "Death by a Thousand Cuts" — Sequencing Note

The pressure in this case is not one smoking gun; it is the **cumulative, compounding weight of legitimate discovery**, sequenced so each production makes the next request unavoidable and each answer narrows the defendants' room to maneuver:

1. **Paper first (months 1–2):** preservation letters (UHSH, Guthrie, ANSCO, Nadeem personally) → complete academic record, complete medical record + EHR audit trail, email account, probation file with metadata, policies. Cheap for us, expensive for them, and every gap vs. the DA-produced set is a new exhibit.
2. **Contention interrogatories (months 2–3):** force AF 6 vs. AF 8 reconciliation act-by-act; factual basis for Ahmed's AF 4/AF 5; identity of every leave-decision participant. Answers here lock positions before anyone testifies.
3. **Third-party subpoenas (months 2–4):** Guthrie (call, posters, CCTV), phone carriers (Nadeem), DA/FOIL, ANSCO once resolved. Third parties have no settlement stake and produce fast; their documents impeach the parties' interrogatory answers.
4. **Depositions last, divergence-ordered (months 4–6, ahead of the November mediation deadline):** 30(b)(6) first (bind the institution), then Ali (signature/forgery — binary), then Rehman (email + audit trail), then Ahmed (separately counseled; positioned to blame the institution), then Nadeem (documentary net already closed). Each transcript feeds the next; contradictions between the two defense groups are independently exploitable.
5. **Mediation posture:** arrive with productions in hand (Drazen: no mediation before their discovery responses), the spoliation record built, and the non-monetary asks (record correction, exculpatory letter, licensure/USCIS cooperation) framed as the accountability-and-change resolution the theme promises — with the monetary discussion opened from the **NEGOTIATION POSTURE** anchor but negotiated against the defensible Tier 2 envelope, $4.34M–$16.57M including statutory fees (ESTIMABLE — canonical band, damages_model.json).

**The ethics line — stated plainly and binding on every instrument above:** volume is a *byproduct* of a genuinely broad wrong, never a tactic in itself. Every single request must be independently justifiable as relevant and proportional under Rule 26(b)(1), signed in good faith under Rule 26(g), and serving a real evidentiary purpose in this map. Nothing is served to harass, burden, embarrass, or run up costs; anything that cannot be defended on relevance at a meet-and-confer does not get served. The pressure is that the requests are all legitimate — that is also what keeps them, and us, credible with the magistrate and the mediator.
