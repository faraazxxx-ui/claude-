## Conventions (binding on every use of this document)

- **Dollar labels.** Every dollar figure carries **DOCUMENTED** (instrument/record identified), **ESTIMABLE** (quantifiable from identified but uncompiled records), or **ASSERTED** (client statement only). Statutory caps are marked **STATUTORY**.
- **Negotiation posture.** The **$100M anchor / $40M acceptance threshold** figures appearing below are **NEGOTIATION POSTURE ONLY** per client directive. They are not pleaded damages and are not supported by the canonical damages model (Tier 2 realistic trial range **$4.34M–$16.57M ESTIMABLE** — compensatory $2.09M–$6.7M, punitive $2M–$8M conditional — per `damages_model.json` / `strategy/damages-architecture.md`, the **single** authoritative band for this package; the earlier r4-synthesis envelope is superseded and survives only as a one-line historical cross-check inside damages-architecture.md). **Rule 11 / mediator-credibility caveat:** these numbers must never appear in a pleading, Rule 26 disclosure, or expert report, and any mediation use requires counsel approval and immediate tie-back to documented components.
- **Citations.** Every case cited is VERIFIED on CourtListener per `data/research/r1–r4` (CL id given). Statutes and anything marked **[unverified — confirm with counsel]** are UNVERIFIED_WEB or NOT-IN-CORPUS items and may not be relied on until verified.
- **No outcome guarantees.** All merits language is probabilistic ("likely," "contestable," "plausible"). Nothing here predicts or promises a result.
- **Theme discipline.** Every counter is deployed inside the case theme: *we are on the right side of this — the goal is accountability and sustainable change, not punishment.* The client's true first-order objective is a **clean academic record, an open licensure path, and a USCIS-safe narrative**; counters that jeopardize those (scorched-earth postures, uncredible numbers) are themselves obstacles.
