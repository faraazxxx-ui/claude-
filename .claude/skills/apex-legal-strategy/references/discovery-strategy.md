## Sequencing Rationale

1. **Written discovery first** — contention interrogatories force both groups to commit to specific facts under oath before depositions.
2. **Ali before Ahmed** — the PD's probation-process testimony frames the institutional narrative; Ahmed then faces a fixed backdrop.
3. **30(b)(6) mid-sequence** — once individuals have committed, the designee must reconcile or own the contradictions.
4. **Funding jurisdiction early** — Title VI depends on federal financial assistance; Document Request 12 should go out with the first set.
5. **Instigation chain continuously** — every witness gets the Sundas-family communications question; malicious prosecution SOL (~Sept 2026) governs the clock.
