## The Gold Nuggets

### 1. Ahmed's Critical Contradiction (Complaint ¶3 vs. Answer ¶2)

Complaint ¶3 alleges Ahmed "held a position of authority and/or influence affecting plaintiff's employment" as "a member in the teaching faculty in the UHS program." He admits the association, denies the influence.

**Exploit**: Force him to define "formerly associated," enumerate his actual duties, and depose other witnesses on his real role. Discovery gold.

### 2. Ahmed's ¶10 Admission

An admission "upon information and belief" that likely concedes the engagement background — anchoring Chain 1's starting node.

### 3. UHSH Same-Decision Defense (AF 7) — The Implicit Admission

This is the **Mt. Healthy / Price Waterhouse mixed-motive defense**. Asserting the actions "would have been reached for legitimate business reasons" implicitly concedes (a) adverse actions occurred and (b) impermissible motives may have existed. Deposition script: "You're saying you WOULD HAVE taken these actions for legitimate reasons — so you acknowledge the actions occurred?"

### 4. AF 6 vs. AF 8 — Internal Contradiction

AF 6 (legitimate business decisions) and AF 8 (outside scope of employment, unauthorized) cannot both be true. If individuals acted outside authority, UHSH cannot claim legitimate business decisions; if legitimate business decisions, the acts were within scope. **Pin this down in interrogatories.**
