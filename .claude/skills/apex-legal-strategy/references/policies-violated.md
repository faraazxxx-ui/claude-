## Full Mapping Table

| Policy/Regulation | Obligation | Violation | Evidence Needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Title VI (42 U.S.C. § 2000d)** | No discrimination on basis of national origin in federally funded programs | Pattern of differential treatment based on Indian origin | Federal funding records, comparative treatment data |
| **ACGME Common Program Requirements** | Due process before adverse actions; fair evaluation; work environment free from discrimination | Probation without notice, hearing, or appeal; unilateral PD signature | ACGME correspondence, probation procedures vs. what happened |
| **FMLA (29 U.S.C. § 2601 et seq.)** | 12 weeks protected leave for serious health condition | Interference with leave rights; failure to provide required notices | FMLA paperwork, communications during leave |
| **ADA (42 U.S.C. § 12101 et seq.)** | Reasonable accommodation for disability; no adverse action based on disability | Forced to work with PIC line and cardiac device; no accommodation offered | Medical communications to program, response (or lack thereof) |
| **HIPAA (45 C.F.R. Part 164)** | Protected health information must not be disclosed without authorization | Medical information disclosed to Sundas's family | Witnesses, communications showing disclosure |
| **FERPA (20 U.S.C. § 1232g)** | Educational records protected from unauthorized disclosure | Academic records shared with third parties | Same as HIPAA evidence |
| **UHS Equal Opportunity Policy** | Internal commitment to non-discrimination (incorporated into employment) | Systematic discrimination violating own policy | Employee handbook, policy documents |
| **UHS Resident Handbook** | Specific procedures for probation, evaluation, grievances | Procedures not followed for probation decision | Handbook vs. actual process comparison |
| **NY Human Rights Law (Exec. Law § 296)** | Prohibition on employment discrimination | Same acts as Title VI claim, under state law | Same evidence |

## Drafting Notes

- Title VI jurisdiction depends on UHS's receipt of federal financial assistance — **must be established in discovery** (Interrogatory 6; Document Request 12 in discovery-strategy.md).
- For the contract claim, quote handbook language verbatim; mandatory terms ("shall") create obligations, aspirational terms ("strives to") do not.
- ACGME violations do double duty: they support both the injunction (records) and the narrative of institutional lawlessness.
