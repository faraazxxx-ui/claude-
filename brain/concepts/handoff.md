# Handoff

**The short version: the reply in the chat window is not the deliverable. Every substantive session
ends in an artifact or a PDF, and the chat message beside it is three lines pointing at it. He is a
visual-spatial thinker with ADHD who arrives with hand-drawn flowcharts and verbose speech, and
leaves needing a picture and one next action. Prose walls and engineering vocabulary are the two
ways to waste his attention.**

---

## The rule

Stated by him on 2026-08-08, and standing until he says otherwise:

1. **The output is an artifact or a PDF.** Not a long chat reply. If the work is worth doing it is
   worth having an address.
2. **At handoff, be short.** The internal plan may be as long as it needs to be. What reaches him
   must not be. Length is a cost paid out of his attention.
3. **Diagrams, not paragraphs, wherever a diagram will carry it.** He thinks in flowcharts. Give
   him back the form he thinks in.
4. **No engineering vocabulary as the headline.** Name the thing in his language and put the term of
   art in small type beside it, if at all. `top-p` is a footnote; *trimming the tail of unlikely
   outcomes* is the heading.
5. **Say it in one word rather than five.** He reads a short label faster than he reads a sentence,
   and he trusts it more.

## The archetype it serves

| Trait | What it means for output |
|---|---|
| Physician | He will act on it. Mark measured, inferred and guessed separately — hard rule 4. |
| ADHD | Initiation is the expensive step. One next action, never a menu. |
| Visual-spatial | A ranked chart beats a ranked list. A drawn mechanism beats its description. |
| Verbal on input | He dictates fast and long. Do not mirror that back; compress it. |
| Time-poor | Scannable in ninety seconds, with the detail underneath for when he wants it. |

## How to tell you got it wrong

The failure is legible before he says anything:

- The reply is longer than the thing it describes.
- The first paragraph explains what you are about to do instead of doing it.
- A table appears where a picture would have been faster.
- A term appears that he would have to look up to act on.
- He has to read to the end to find what to do next.

## Why this is a page and not a preference

He has explained this more than once, across sessions that could not see each other — the same
correction, re-issued. That is the exact failure [[wiki-method]] exists to stop. It is written here
so it is told once. `CLAUDE.md` carries the one-line version and points at this page.
