## **PETITION SUMMARY**

This petition challenges the accreditation of the UHS Wilson Medical Center Internal Medicine Residency Program based on **documented violations of ACGME Institutional and Program Requirements**, specifically:

1. **FMLA Obstruction and Due Process Violations** (ACGME Common Program Requirement VI.C.1)
2. **IT Access Denial and Academic Record Withholding** (ACGME Institutional Requirement III.B.2)

These violations created a **hostile educational environment** that compromised patient safety, violated resident rights, and breached the trust placed in ACGME-accredited programs.

## **III. FACTUAL ALLEGATIONS**

### **A. FMLA Obstruction and Due Process Violations**

#### **1. Medical Certification Fraud**

**Documented Evidence:**
- **FMLA Certification PDF** (Doug/6. FMLA/ folder): Direct email to Dr. Ali regarding Brugada Syndrome diagnosis, PICC line placement, and ongoing harassment
- **12-Week Medical Documentation**: Comprehensive medical records supporting need for protected leave
- **NY State FMLA Rights Documentation**: Legal framework for protected leave

**Violations:**
- Program leadership **ignored medical certifications** and forced resident to work while septic with PICC line
- **Denied legitimate FMLA leave** despite qualifying medical conditions (Brugada Syndrome, post-surgical infection)
- **Imposed probation without due process** during protected FMLA period (Late April 2024)

**ACGME Requirement Breach:**
> "Residents must have access to a fair and expeditious process for resolving disputes regarding their evaluation or treatment within the program." — **Common Program Requirement §VI.C.1**

**Patient Safety Impact:**
- Forcing a septic resident with active PICC line to perform clinical duties created **direct patient risk**
- Violated **standard of care** and **infection control protocols**

## **IV. LEGAL AND ETHICAL IMPLICATIONS**

### **A. Pattern of Institutional Retaliation**

The documented actions demonstrate a **systematic campaign of retaliation** following the breakdown of a personal relationship between the petitioner and the daughter of Dr. Awais Ahmed, a teaching faculty member. This creates a **conflict of interest** that compromised the educational environment.

**Key Evidence:**
- Audio Recording (Exhibit A): "We knew it is false. We framed him" — **Proves actual malice**
- Bodycam Footage (Exhibit B): References contacting "doctors at UHS" — **Proves pre-coordination**
- Grandfather Testimony (Exhibit D): Establishes **personal motive for fabrication**

### **B. Cat's Paw Liability Application**

Under **Cat's Paw theory**, UHS adopted a **personal vendetta** as institutional policy without independent investigation. The program leadership became complicit in the retaliation by:

1. **Failing to investigate** the false allegations
2. **Imposing disciplinary actions** without due process
3. **Withholding exculpatory evidence** (negative drug tests)
4. **Continuing retaliation** after criminal acquittal (August 2025)
