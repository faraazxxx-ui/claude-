## ARTICLE 2 — NATURE OF THE TRANSACTION: A LOAN, NOT A GIFT

2.1 **Classification.** This Agreement documents a *qarḍ ḥasan* — a benevolent, strictly interest-free loan. **Every Advance is a loan from Lender to Borrower.** No Advance is, or shall later be recharacterized as, a gift, wage, investment, or partnership contribution.

2.2 **Presumption of Loan.** Any transfer of money from Lender to Borrower during the term of this Agreement is conclusively presumed to be an Advance under this Agreement **unless**, at or before the time of transfer, both Parties sign a written designation that the specific transfer is a gift (*hibah*). Oral statements, family custom, or later recollection shall not overcome this presumption.

2.3 **Prior Advances.** The transfers listed in **Schedule B** (the "Prior Advances"), made by the Lender to or for the benefit of the Borrower before the Effective Date, are hereby acknowledged by both Parties as loans (*qarḍ*) from inception, are incorporated into the principal owed under this Agreement, and are governed by its terms.

2.4 **Absolute Prohibition of Ribā.** The Borrower's repayment obligation is limited to the exact aggregate principal of the Advances — no more and no less. No interest, late fee, penalty, premium, or other increase payable to or retained by the Lender shall be charged, paid, or accepted, whether stipulated or customary. Any provision or practice to the contrary is void *ab initio*. Voluntary excess offered freely by the Borrower at settlement, without prior stipulation or expectation, is not prohibited but may never be demanded or presumed.

## ARTICLE 3 — LENDER'S COVENANT: FIXED, PUNCTUAL SUPPORT

3.1 **Amount.** The Lender shall advance to the Borrower the Support Installment (§ 1.4) in each Payment Period. The amount is **fixed** and does not vary with the Lender's discretion, the state of family relations, or the progress of the Civil Action.

3.2 **Schedule.** Installments are due **every fourteen (14) days**, on ____________ (day of week), beginning on ____________________, 20____ (the "**First Due Date**"). A schedule of the first ten due dates appears in **Schedule A**.

3.3 **Punctuality.** Timely payment is of the essence of this Agreement, the Parties having agreed to it as a binding condition (*"Muslims are bound by their conditions"*; Sūrah al-Mā'idah 5:1). Each installment shall be initiated so as to arrive **on or before its due date**. A grace period of three (3) business days applies; payment after the grace period is a delay.

3.4 **Notice of Delay.** If the Lender anticipates that any installment will be delayed, he shall notify the Borrower **before the due date**, stating the reason and the new date, which shall be no later than seven (7) days after the original due date.

3.5 **Cumulation; No Forfeiture.** A delayed or missed installment is not extinguished. It remains due and shall be paid together with, or before, the next installment. Delay by the Lender does not accrue any penalty, but repeated unexcused delay is a breach of this Agreement.

3.6 **Method.** Installments shall be sent by wire transfer or Zelle to the Borrower's designated account: ____________________________. Each transfer shall carry the memo/reference "**QARD-[sequence number]**" so it can be matched to the Ledger.

3.7 **Duration.** The Lender's obligation to make installments continues from the First Due Date until the **earliest** of: (a) Final Resolution; (b) the date the Borrower commences employment at an annual salary equal to or greater than the Reference Salary; or (c) written termination signed by both Parties. *(Optional offset — initial to adopt: if the Borrower earns income during the term, each installment is reduced dollar-for-dollar by the income received in that Payment Period. ## ARTICLE 4 — BORROWER'S COVENANT: UNCONDITIONAL REPAYMENT

4.1 **The Debt.** The Borrower owes the Lender the aggregate principal of all Advances as recorded in the Ledger (the "**Debt**"). **The Debt is unconditional.** It is owed in full whether the Civil Action is won, lost, settled, or dismissed. The outcome of the Civil Action fixes the *timing* and *accounting* of repayment — never *whether* repayment is owed.

4.2 **Maturity.** The Debt is due and payable on the **Maturity Date** (ninety (90) days after Final Resolution).

4.3 **Longstop.** If Final Resolution has not occurred by ____________________, 20____ (the "**Longstop Date**"), the Parties shall confer in good faith within thirty (30) days to set a repayment schedule. Absent written agreement, the Debt becomes payable in equal quarterly installments over twenty-four (24) months beginning ninety (90) days after the Longstop Date.

4.4 **Source of Repayment.** Repayment shall be made **first** from the Net Recovery, within the ninety (90) day period following its receipt. Any shortfall remains owed and shall be paid from the Borrower's other resources under the schedule in § 4.2–4.3.

4.5 **Hardship Respite.** If at maturity the Borrower is genuinely unable to pay, the Parties shall apply Sūrah al-Baqarah 2:280: *"If the debtor is in hardship, grant him respite until ease."* The Lender may extend time or, solely at his own free choice, remit part or all of the Debt as charity; remission shall never be presumed, requested as of right, or inferred from silence, and is effective only in a signed writing.

4.6 **Prepayment.** The Borrower may repay all or part of the Debt at any time without penalty, and is encouraged to do so (*"The best of people are those who are best in repaying their debts"*).

4.7 **Estate Obligations.** The Debt binds the Borrower's estate; the Lender's rights pass to his heirs under applicable law and Islamic inheritance principles. Death of either Party neither extinguishes nor accelerates the Debt except as the law of the estate requires.

## ARTICLE 5 — THE LEDGER: THE ACCURATE INVENTORY

5.1 **Maintenance.** The Borrower, as scribe (*kātib*), shall maintain the Ledger recording, for **every** Advance: date sent, date received, amount, method, bank reference number, memo code (§ 3.6), running principal total, and a SHA-256 hash chaining each entry to the last — consistent with the chain-of-custody practice already used for the Parties' banking records in the Civil Action.

5.2 **Expenditure Inventory.** The Borrower shall also record, for each Payment Period, the categories in which Advances were spent (housing, medical, legal, living, transportation, other), so that the Ledger constitutes an accurate inventory of **every amount sent and every amount spent**, suitable for use in the damages accounting of the Civil Action.

5.3 **Monthly Reconciliation.** Within seven (7) days after each calendar month, the Borrower shall deliver the month's Ledger entries to the Lender. The Lender shall confirm or dispute each entry within fourteen (14) days; entries not disputed in writing within that period are deemed accepted by both Parties.

5.4 **Conclusive Evidence.** The reconciled Ledger is conclusive evidence between the Parties of the amount of the Debt, absent manifest error or fraud.

5.5 **Format.** The Ledger shall follow the column format in **Schedule C** (template file: `Qard_Ledger_Template.csv`).

## ARTICLE 6 — FORMALITIES OF SŪRAH AL-BAQARAH 2:282

6.1 **Writing at the Borrower's Dictation.** This Agreement was reduced to writing at the instance and dictation of the **Borrower** — the party who owes the Debt — in fulfillment of the verse's command that *"the one who owes the debt dictate."*

6.2 **Witnesses.** This Agreement shall be attested by **two adult witnesses** of sound mind (or as the verse alternatively provides, one man and two women), who witness the Parties' signatures and may be called upon to testify. *"And do not conceal testimony, for whoever conceals it — his heart is sinful."* (2:283)

6.3 **No Harm to Scribe or Witness.** Neither Party shall pressure, harm, or retaliate against the scribe or any witness (2:282: *"Let no scribe be harmed, nor any witness"*).

6.4 **Whether Small or Large.** The Parties acknowledge the verse's instruction not to neglect writing any debt, *"whether small or large, together with its term,"* and accordingly agree that **no future family advance is exempt from documentation by reason of its size or the closeness of their relationship.**

## ARTICLE 7 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

7.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict-of-laws rules. The Parties intend its terms to be construed, wherever the language permits, consistently with the Islamic principles recited herein; if any Islamic-law recital is unenforceable as law, it remains binding on the Parties' consciences and the operative civil terms stand alone.

7.2 **Dispute Resolution.** Disputes shall be addressed in this order: (a) direct, good-faith discussion between father and son; (b) mediation before a mutually acceptable person of knowledge and standing (which may include an imām or Islamic scholar); (c) the courts of the State of New York or the federal courts sitting therein, to whose jurisdiction the Parties consent.

7.3 **Entire Agreement; Amendment.** This Agreement (with its Schedules) is the entire agreement of the Parties concerning family financial support and supersedes all prior oral understandings. It may be amended only in a writing signed by both Parties and witnessed as in § 6.2.

7.4 **Severability.** If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder stands, and the unenforceable provision shall be reformed to the closest enforceable equivalent.

7.5 **Notices.** Notices shall be in writing (including email or text message with delivery confirmation) to the addresses/numbers set out beneath the signatures.

7.6 **No Partnership or Agency.** Nothing herein creates a partnership, agency, employment, or investment relationship. The sole relationship created is lender and borrower.

7.7 **Counterparts; Electronic Signature.** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original.
