## What the contract does (the two things you asked for)

1. **Fixes the amount and the clock on Dad's side.** The Support Installment is a fixed dollar figure pegged to the bi-weekly salary under your original contract, due **every 14 days on a named day**, with a 3-business-day grace period, a duty to give notice *before* a due date if a payment will be late, and no forfeiture — a missed installment stays owed (Article 3).
2. **Fixes the repayment on your side.** Every dollar is a **loan (qarḍ), never a gift** — conclusively presumed (Article 2). Repayment is principal-only (no ribā), due 90 days after Final Resolution of the civil case, paid **first from the Net Recovery**, and tracked through the same accurate inventory (hash-chained ledger of every amount sent and spent) already used in your evidence practice (Articles 4–5).

One deliberate design decision to understand: **the Debt is unconditional** (§ 4.1). The case outcome controls *when* and *from what source* you repay — never *whether* you owe. This is required on both tracks: a repayment contingent on winning is not a valid qarḍ in fiqh (it becomes gharar/speculation), and a "loan" only repayable on victory is exactly what defense counsel would use to recharacterize the wires as gifts and knock them out of your damages.
