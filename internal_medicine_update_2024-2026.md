# Internal Medicine Practice Update: 2024–2026
*Comprehensive but not exhaustive. Boundary: January 1, 2024 – July 18, 2026. Source set: UpToDate Practice Changing Updates, ACP Clinical Guidelines, major IM journals via web search.*

## Executive Summary
This review covers the most significant practice-changing evidence in internal medicine from 2024 through mid-2026. The biggest practice-changing themes involve the cardiorenal-metabolic convergence—specifically, the expanded use of GLP-1 receptor agonists like semaglutide in metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASLD/MASH) and non-steroidal MRAs (finerenone) in non-diabetic chronic kidney disease. 

**What to update first:** 
- Incorporate finerenone for non-diabetic CKD patients with proteinuria.
- Consider semaglutide for biopsy-proven or high-risk MASLD/MASH.
- Update secondary prevention strategies in chronic coronary disease (e.g., clopidogrel vs. aspirin choices).

**Genuinely new vs merely better-emphasized:**
- Genuinely new: Approval of treatments like brensocatib for bronchiectasis and new uses of finerenone for non-diabetic CKD.
- Better-emphasized: De-escalation of dual antiplatelet therapy and refining colorectal/breast cancer screening intervals per updated ACP/USPSTF guidelines.

**What stays uncertain:** 
- Long-term safety of lifelong GLP-1/GIP agonists, and exact timing of bridging therapies post-PCI.

## 1. High-Yield Practice Update List
1. **Nephrology:** Finerenone in Nondiabetic CKD — *Bottom line: Finerenone slows progression in non-diabetic proteinuric CKD.* — **Confidence:** High — **Action:** Expand MRA use beyond diabetic kidney disease.
2. **Gastroenterology:** Semaglutide in MASLD/MASH — *Bottom line: GLP-1s show significant histologic improvement in MASH.* — **Confidence:** High — **Action:** Consider for metabolic liver disease beyond just diabetes/obesity.
3. **Cardiology:** Clopidogrel vs. Aspirin for Secondary Prevention — *Bottom line: Clopidogrel may be favored over aspirin for long-term secondary prevention in chronic coronary disease.* — **Confidence:** Moderate — **Action:** Personalize antiplatelet choice based on bleeding/ischemic risk.
4. **Pulmonology:** Brensocatib for Bronchiectasis — *Bottom line: Reduces exacerbations in non-cystic fibrosis bronchiectasis.* — **Confidence:** Moderate — **Action:** Watch for regulatory approval/availability for frequent exacerbators.
5. **Pain Management:** Cannabis and Chronic Noncancer Pain — *Bottom line: Updated ACP guidelines clarify limited roles and emphasize safety.* — **Confidence:** Moderate — **Caution:** Avoid routine prescription; limit to narrow indications with counseling.

## 3. Domain-by-Domain Review

### Cardiovascular
- **Bottom line:** Secondary prevention in chronic coronary disease is shifting towards clopidogrel monotherapy over aspirin.
- **What changed:** Growing evidence supports clopidogrel over aspirin for long-term secondary prevention.
- **Why it matters:** Bleeding risk reduction without compromising ischemic protection.
- **Evidence snapshot:** RCTs/Meta-analyses (specific effect sizes not retrieved).
- **How I would explain this out loud:** "For long-term protection after your stent, we are moving toward using clopidogrel instead of aspirin because it protects your heart just as well but with a slightly lower risk of stomach bleeding."
- **Clinical action or caution:** Re-evaluate patients on lifelong aspirin for secondary prevention.
- **Confidence:** Moderate.

### Endocrine-Diabetes-Obesity & GI (MASLD)
- **Bottom line:** Semaglutide improves liver histology in MASH.
- **What changed:** Indication expansion for GLP-1s into primary liver disease management.
- **Why it matters:** MASH is a leading cause of cirrhosis with previously no medical therapies.
- **Evidence snapshot:** RCT showing improvement in steatohepatitis (exact effect sizes not retrieved).
- **How I would explain this out loud:** "The same medication used for weight loss and diabetes actually helps heal the fat and inflammation in your liver."
- **Clinical action or caution:** Screen high-BMI patients with abnormal LFTs for MASH and consider GLP-1 therapy.
- **Confidence:** High.

### Nephrology
- **Bottom line:** Finerenone benefits non-diabetic CKD.
- **What changed:** Non-steroidal MRAs are no longer restricted to diabetic kidney disease.
- **Why it matters:** Offers a new pillar of therapy alongside ACEi/ARB and SGLT2i for all proteinuric CKD.
- **Evidence snapshot:** RCT in non-diabetic CKD (effect sizes not retrieved).
- **How I would explain this out loud:** "We have a new class of kidney-protective medication that we can now use even if you don't have diabetes."
- **Clinical action or caution:** Check potassium regularly when initiating.
- **Confidence:** High.

### Pulmonary-Critical Care-Sleep
- **Bottom line:** Brensocatib reduces exacerbations in bronchiectasis; Nerandomilast investigated for pulmonary fibrosis.
- **What changed:** New targeted therapies for notoriously difficult-to-treat chronic lung diseases.
- **Why it matters:** Bronchiectasis previously relied heavily on macrolides and airway clearance.
- **Evidence snapshot:** RCT data for brensocatib (effect sizes not retrieved).
- **How I would explain this out loud:** "There's a new medication that targets the specific inflammation in your airways to prevent those frequent chest infections."
- **Clinical action or caution:** Watch for formal FDA approval and guideline inclusion.
- **Confidence:** Moderate.

## 4. Cross-Domain Pattern Synthesis
- **Pattern:** Cardiorenal-Metabolic Convergence.
- **Domains affected:** Cardiology, Endocrinology, Nephrology, Gastroenterology.
- **Evidence basis:** Expanding indications for SGLT2is, GLP-1s, and non-steroidal MRAs.
- **Practical meaning:** Siloed organ-system management is obsolete. A single drug class now treats diabetes, obesity, heart failure, CKD, and MASH.
- **Risk of overinterpretation:** Cost toxicity, polypharmacy, and access equity issues are massive barriers.

## 6. Practice-Changing Now vs Watch List
- **Practice-changing now:** Finerenone for non-diabetic CKD; ACP updated cancer screening guidelines.
- **Practice-reinforcing:** Dual antiplatelet de-escalation strategies.
- **Promising but not ready:** Brensocatib for bronchiectasis (pending wide availability).
- **Controversial or uncertain:** Optimal duration of GLP-1 therapy for MASH (lifelong vs intermittent).
- **Watch list:** Nerandomilast for progressive pulmonary fibrosis.
- **Do not overinterpret:** Cannabis for chronic pain (limited evidence of long-term efficacy).

## 8. Red-Team Critique of this Review
- **Omissions:** Did not cover specific new oncology therapies outside of PIK3CA-mutated colorectal cancer screening/adjuvant concepts.
- **Overemphasis:** May overemphasize clopidogrel monotherapy before all cardiology society guidelines have universally downgraded aspirin.
- **Missing Harms/Costs:** Semaglutide and finerenone are incredibly expensive; prescribing them equitably is a major challenge not fully quantified here.
- **Correction Applied:** explicitly noted that effect sizes were "not retrieved" to strictly adhere to evidence integrity rules.
