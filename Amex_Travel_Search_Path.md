# Amex Travel Portal — Exact Search Path (The Fifth Avenue Hotel, Sept 12)

## Direct link (preferred)

1. Go to **https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/fine-hotels-and-resorts/**
2. Sign in with your Amex Platinum-linked account.
3. Search:
   - **Destination:** New York, NY
   - **Check-in:** 2026-09-12
   - **Check-out:** 2026-09-13
   - **Guests:** 2 adults, 1 room
4. Filter/Program: **Fine Hotels + Resorts** (toggle on)
5. Find **"The Fifth Avenue Hotel"** in the results (NoMad neighborhood).
6. Confirm the listed benefits include: **daily breakfast for two · $100 property credit · room upgrade when available · guaranteed 4pm checkout · complimentary Wi-Fi**.
7. **Confirm the complimentary valet parking inclusion** — it should appear in the property's specific FHR benefit list; if not visible, call Amex Travel (number on the portal) and ask them to confirm the parking inclusion for The Fifth Avenue Hotel FHR rate before you pay.

## Pay with

Your **Amex Platinum Card** — this is what activates the FHR benefits *and* earns the up-to-$600/year FHR statement credit.

## Do NOT use

The Founders Card Synxis link (https://be.synxis.com/?...&promo=FoundersCard2026...) for this booking — it gives a discounted rate but **no free parking**. FHR is the stronger channel here because parking is the single biggest cost the family would otherwise pay.
