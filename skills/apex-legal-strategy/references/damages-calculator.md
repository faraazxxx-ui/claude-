## Methodology Notes

- Update figures before any settlement demand or amended complaint; stale damages undermine credibility.
- Lost salary is per-year ($300K/yr) — state the duration assumption explicitly in every calculation.
- Punitive range 1:1 to 3:1 against compensatory; justify multiplier by egregiousness (fabricated complaints, forced PIC-line work, record falsification).
- Every figure must trace to a document in the evidence inventory — no orphan numbers in any filing.
