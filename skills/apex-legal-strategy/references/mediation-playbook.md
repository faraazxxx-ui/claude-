# Mediation Playbook — November 2026 (distilled)

**Canon**: `legal-war-room/mediation/mediation-execution-plan.md` (dated backward-plan, scripts,
concession ladder) + `demand-framework.md` (demand skeleton) + `action-schedule.md` (paced prep).

## Sequencing (Drazen's rule + ours)
No mediation before defendants' discovery responses. Backward-plan from Nov: omnibus amendment (MP both
rails + NYSHRL/§296(6) + FMLA) lands first — it is what makes the damages architecture real; then the
keystone productions (academic record, ANSCO CCTV, Nadeem texts, Guthrie call/poster file, DA file).

## Opening structure
1. **Non-monetary package first** (record correction, neutral reference, licensure/USCIS support letter,
   ACGME-safe language): cheap for defendants, priceless for the client, momentum-builder — and it is the
   client's actual first-order objective.
2. **Exposure conversation** with defense counsel (not the mediator's opening): composite trial risk
   (≥1 significant claim trial-ready ≈ 80–90%), fee-shifting accrual per month of delay, the labeled
   exposure ceiling with its caveat script.
3. **Number choreography**: anchor → ladder rungs (each concession buys a return) → acceptance threshold
   ($40M posture — counsel owns the client gap) → defensible zone ($5M–$12M post-discovery) → floor/BATNA.

## Credibility rules (binding)
- Never present a POSTURE figure without its label and caveat; one called bluff reprices everything.
- The mediator gets the verified arithmetic (Tier 2) and the honesty ledger — that is what buys belief in
  the rest.
- Adverse authority is disclosed in our own risk framing before the defense raises it (*Cummings*, *Colon*
  presumption, academic deference) — with the counter attached.

## Walk-away
Trial posture is genuinely acceptable: fee-shifting claims accrue monthly, the §1983 rail is alive to
~2028, and every month of discovery deepens the contradiction record. Walking away is a move, not a
failure — provided the non-monetary package has been priced separately and preserved.
