## Anti-double-count discipline
Paternal loans (~$900K) and family living expenses (~$1M) corroborate the SAME loss as the salary lines —
carried `in_totals=false` as corroboration, never added on top. State the duration assumption on every
per-year figure.

## Key constraints (verified)
- Title VI: economic + equitable + fees only (*Cummings* 596 U.S. 212; *Barnes* 536 U.S. 181).
- NYSHRL (post-10/2019): uncapped compensatory + punitives vs private employers (Exec. L. §297(9)); *Chauca*
  standard is NYCHRL-only — do not cite it as NYSHRL authority without the 2019-amendment bridge.
- No punitive on the contract count (*Rocanova*); tortious-interference punitive needs NY moral-turpitude
  showing (*Walker v. Sheldon*).
- Defendant wealth: UHSH ~$1.16B Binghamton non-profit — never import Universal Health Services verdicts.
- Upgrade path: every record obtained (executed FL contract, wires, pharmacy, economist, psych) ratchets a
  line's label up and moves the defensible mid — update the model, then the win-probability doc.
