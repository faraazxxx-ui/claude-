# Story frameworks (Layer 2 narrative)

## SCQH for litigation
- **Situation:** who the client was before the wrong (competence, trajectory, stakes).
- **Complication:** the wrong — as a causal chain with dates, not a list of grievances.
- **Question:** the single question the forum must answer (keep it one sentence).
- **Hypothesis/Resolution:** the relief that answers it — and why it is reasonable, bounded, and repair-shaped.

## Arc discipline
The strongest plaintiff arc is **competence → coordinated harm → exoneration → unrepaired damage → this
case as the repair**. Exoneration beats grievance: lead with what neutral actors already concluded
(acquittal, reversals, internal disciplinary actions against the accusers) before what the client alleges.
Restraint is credibility: the ask framed as accountability + record repair + "so it doesn't happen to the
next person" outperforms punishment framing with every audience.

## Audience shifts (same facts, different lens)
| Audience | Lead with | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Mediator | Risk exposure + reasonableness of the ask; the caveat-boxed number ladder | Uncaveated anchors; moral outrage |
| Defense counsel | Their contradictions and discovery exposure; cost of each additional month | Bluffs they can call |
| Jury | The human chronology; one exhibit per beat; restraint | Legal jargon; damages arithmetic too early |

## Beat anchoring
Every beat carries its anchor inline (timestamp, exhibit, docket entry). A beat with no anchor is cut or
marked as allegation. The cold-open version (one page) is written last and contains only anchored beats.

## Zero-sum posture, honestly run
Anchoring high is legitimate negotiation; lying is not. The posture number lives in a labeled tier with its
assumptions listed; the acceptance threshold sits at the top of the defensible trial range; the floor is
documented damages + the non-monetary must-haves. When an obstacle appears, generate the closest viable
counter before conceding ground — and record obstacles you cannot counter in the matrix rather than hiding
them. The projection of victory is a maintained probability document, not a slogan.
