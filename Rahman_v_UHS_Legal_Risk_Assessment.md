- **Question:** How should plaintiff sequence offensive filings and discovery to convert the strong merits position into recovery before the deadline window closes?
- **Hypothesis:** File the amended complaint (malicious prosecution + FMLA + ADA + defamation/IIED) immediately to beat the SOL, then drive discovery to lock the four "gold nugget" contradictions before defendants can repair their positions.
